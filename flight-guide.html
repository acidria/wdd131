Project Plan

Subject: Basic Flight Instruments and Altimetry Guide

Content: The planned site will introduce the airspeed indicator, altimeter, attitude indicator, and heading indicator. It will also explain the difference between QNH and standard pressure at a general level. A later version can include an interactive topic selector or unit converter.

Reason: Aviation interests me, and these topics are easy to organize into clear sections. They will let me practice semantic HTML, responsive CSS, and JavaScript events without making the project too complicated.
